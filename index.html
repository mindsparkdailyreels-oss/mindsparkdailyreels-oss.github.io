A new short clip every day about science, space, engineering and surprising facts.

What we do

Mind Spark Daily publishes short vertical videos on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Clips come from Creative Commons (CC BY) videos, credited to their creators, and from brand and creator campaigns that give permission to clip their videos.

reel-bot

reel-bot is the private tool we use to prepare and publish these clips to our own Mind Spark Daily YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube API Services only to upload videos to that channel and to read the channel's basic details. It is not offered to other users.
